## 2. Title

**Use:** `Cassini Found Something Very Strange on Saturn's Moon Iapetus` (59 characters)

Alternatives, for a title A/B test if your channel has it, or for a re-title after 48 hours:
- `The Moon With Two Faces and a Wall Around Its Middle`
- `Why Does Saturn's Moon Iapetus Have Two Faces?`

Why the lead title: it names a real mission (borrowed authority), names the place (search), and leaves the "strange" part open. The thumbnail then shows it.

## 3. Thumbnails: run a 3-way test

In YouTube Studio go to **Details → Thumbnail → Test & Compare** and upload A, B and C. YouTube splits impressions and picks the winner by watch time, usually within about two weeks.

| | Concept | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | The half-black, half-white moon, filling the frame | The visual mystery is obvious instantly; "TWO FACES?" invites the click |
| **B** | Surface view of the ridge as a giant wall, with Saturn above | Scale shock; "20 KM HIGH" is a concrete, true number |
| **C** | Edge-on moon with the ridge glowing as a seam | The open question; "NOBODY KNOWS WHY" is true, since there's no scientific consensus |

If Test & Compare isn't available on your channel yet, start with **A** and swap to B after 3–4 days if click-through rate is under about 4%.

The clean versions let you add your own text in Canva or Photoshop, and are useful for community posts.

## 4. Description, tags and chapters

- **Description:** paste `description.txt`. It already has chapter timestamps, the AI-visuals disclosure, sources and 3 hashtags. The first two lines are what shows in search, so they carry the hook and keywords.
- **Tags:** paste `tags.txt` (267 characters; the limit is 500). Tags matter little now, but they help with misspellings like "iapitus".
- **Chapters:** YouTube builds them automatically from the timestamps in the description (they start at 0:00, there are more than 3, and each is longer than 10 seconds).

## 8. After publishing

1. **Pin** `pinned-comment.txt` as the first comment. It asks a question, and the replies boost early engagement.
2. **Community post** (once the channel has the tab): use clean thumbnail A with "Which side would you rather land on? 🌑 / ⚪" and a two-option poll.

4. **Schedule:** publish on a weekday between 14:00 and 16:00 in your main audience's time zone (US Eastern is the usual default for English science content), and stay around for the first hour to answer comments.
