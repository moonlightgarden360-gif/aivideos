# Script: The Alien Signal We Never Heard Again (the Wow! signal)

**Format:** Astrum-style alien mystery: honest evidence with an open ending
**Length:** 5:00, as 20 blocks of 15 seconds. Each line is about 28–33 words (≈12–14 s of narration), the same format as the Iapetus video, so it can go straight into the same production pipeline.
**Tone:** calm, curious, a skeptic who takes it seriously. Every claim below is real and sourced.

## Packaging

**Title options**
1. **The Alien Signal We Never Heard Again** (lead)
2. For 47 Years, Nobody Could Explain This Signal
3. Scientists May Finally Know What Sent the "Wow!" Signal (for a re-title after a week)

**Thumbnail:** an old dot-matrix printout with **6EQUJ5** circled in red and a handwritten **"Wow!"**, against a radio telescope at night. Text: **"72 SECONDS"**. The printout text must be added as an overlay after generation, because AI renders letters unreliably.

**Short hook (for the companion Short):** *"In 1977, we received a 72-second signal from space. We've been listening for it ever since."*

**Why this structure works for the alien audience:**
- **0:00–0:45:** maximum mystery, with the alien option presented seriously.
- **2:00–2:45:** the "forbidden frequency" and the "heard once" twist. This is the conspiracy energy, and every word of it is true.
- **3:00–3:30:** debunk attempts that failed, which builds credibility.
- **3:45–4:30:** the real 2024 hypothesis, framed honestly as unproven.
- **4:45:** an open ending. Alien believers and skeptics both feel the video agreed with them, so both camps comment.

## Production notes

- **Pipeline:** identical to the Iapetus video: 20 × 15 s Seedance 2.0 clips, one narrator line per clip, ambient drone, assembled at 1080p. Use `iapetus-5min/assemble.sh` with new URL lists.
- **Text overlays** (6EQUJ5, "Wow!", the signal curve, the spectrum) go in after generation. Don't rely on the video model to draw readable characters.
- **No real people's faces.** Show Jerry Ehman only as hands and paper. Never generate a likeness of a real person.
- **Disclosure:** tick "altered or synthetic content". The description should say the visuals are illustrations.
- **Pronunciation:** "Méndez" may need a phonetic spelling for the voice ("MEN-dez").
